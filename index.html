What does a mortgage agent do?

A mortgage agent compares mortgage options from several lenders for you, helps you choose the one that fits your goals, and handles the application through to closing. Emilie Greller and Aaron Klimitz are licensed Mortgage Agents (Level 1) in Ontario with BRX Mortgage Inc., FSRA licence #13463.

How much do I need for a down payment in Canada?

At least 5% of the first $500,000 of the purchase price and 10% of the portion between $500,000 and $1.5 million. Homes priced at $1.5 million or more need 20% down. With less than 20% down, the mortgage needs default insurance from CMHC, Sagen or Canada Guaranty, and the premium is usually added to the mortgage.

When should I start my mortgage renewal?

About four months (120 days) before your term ends. Many lenders will hold a rate for around that long, which gives you time to compare offers instead of signing the first renewal letter that arrives.

Can I refinance my mortgage to pay off debt or invest?

Usually, if you have enough equity. In Canada you can generally refinance up to 80% of your home's value. The money can pay off higher-interest debt or fund an investment, such as the down payment on another property. Breaking a term early can carry a prepayment penalty, so that gets checked first.

What documents do I need to apply for a mortgage?

Photo ID, proof of income (recent pay stubs and an employment letter, or two years of tax returns and Notices of Assessment if you are self-employed), proof of your down payment, and the purchase agreement if you are buying.

What is the mortgage stress test?

Lenders must check that you could still afford your payments at a higher rate: the greater of your contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%. The calculator above shows your qualifying rate.
